▶Vid Studio

A web studio that helps creators make short vertical videos and publish them to their own channels.

You pick a topic for your channel. Vid Studio writes a short script, voices it, adds pictures and captions and renders a vertical video. You watch it in the Studio, edit the title and caption, and choose where it goes: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, a Facebook Page or Threads.

Nothing is posted without you. For every platform you connect, you sign in with that platform yourself, and for TikTok you choose who can view each video and confirm every post in the Studio.

Creators sign up with an e-mail and a password, connect their own accounts and see their own videos' views and earnings in one place. Vid Studio was first built for the series 365 Businesses That Make Money.
