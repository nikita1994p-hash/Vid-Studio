Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Vid Studio is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It makes short videos and, when the owner asks it to, uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not a hosted service and has no server that collects data from anyone.

What Google data Vid Studio uses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Vid Studio asks Google for permission to:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) — to publish the videos the owner made, with their title, description, tags and cover picture;
  • Read the channel (youtube.readonly) — to show the channel name and the views and likes of the uploaded videos;
  • Manage the uploaded videos (youtube.force-ssl) — only when the owner presses “Publish now” or “Delete from YouTube” for a video Vid Studio uploaded: to make that private video public or to delete it. Vid Studio never changes or deletes any other video;
  • Read YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly) — to show the owner their own views, watch time, subscribers and, once the channel is monetized, revenue.

Where the data is kept

The sign-in tokens and the statistics are stored only in files on the owner's computer. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party, never sold, and never used for advertising. Data is sent only to Google's own APIs, to do what the owner asked.

Vid Studio accounts

To sign in to Vid Studio itself you create an account with your e-mail and a password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash. Your e-mail is used to sign you in and to manage your account.

If, and only if, you tick “Send me news, tips and offers” when you sign up (or later on the My account page), your e-mail and the date you agreed are added to the mailing list for news and offers about Vid Studio. The box is not ticked by default and is not required to use Vid Studio. You can untick it at any time on the My account page or use the unsubscribe link in any e-mail. Your e-mail is never sold or given to third parties for their own marketing.

Limited Use

Vid Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Vid Studio also uses the YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How to remove access

Press Log out on the Accounts page of Vid Studio to delete the stored tokens, and revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Vid Studio folder deletes all data it stored.
